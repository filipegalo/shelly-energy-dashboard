DIAGNOSTICS

Mismatch checklist

  • Check whether a circuit meter and its appliance meters are all configured as primary.
  • Check whether a three-phase total and its individual phases are both included.
  • Check device clocks and the application timezone.
  • Check whether the main meter or a room source was unavailable.
  • Check whether a source counter reset occurred.
  • Check whether a room meter measures exported rather than imported energy.

The application never changes assignment roles automatically from a suspected mismatch.
